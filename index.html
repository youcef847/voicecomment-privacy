Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Welcome to VoiceComment. VoiceComment is a free Android application that allows users to convert spoken words into text while using other applications.

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information VoiceComment accesses, how it is used, and how third-party services may process information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

VoiceComment does not operate its own servers and does not independently collect, upload, or store users' personal information.

We do not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal details.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking technologies.

2. Microphone and Speech Recognition

VoiceComment requires microphone permission to convert your speech into text.

When you activate speech recognition:

  • Your microphone is used to capture your spoken words.
  • Speech recognition is provided through the Android speech recognition service available on your device.
  • Depending on your device, recognition provider, settings, and network connection, audio may be processed locally or transmitted to the speech recognition provider for processing.
  • VoiceComment does not independently upload or store your audio recordings on its own servers.

Speech recognition providers may process audio and related information according to their own privacy policies and practices. We do not control how third-party recognition providers handle information.

Please consult the privacy policy of your device's speech recognition provider for further information.

3. Floating Window Permission

VoiceComment uses Android's display-over-other-apps permission to show a floating bubble over other applications.

This feature allows you to access speech recognition without leaving the application you are currently using.

The floating bubble is used solely to provide VoiceComment's functionality.

4. Clipboard Access

VoiceComment allows you to copy recognized text to your device's clipboard.

Copied text is made available through Android's clipboard functionality so that you can paste it into another application.

VoiceComment does not operate a clipboard collection or monitoring service.

Once text has been copied, its subsequent handling is determined by Android and the application into which you paste it.

5. Language Preferences

VoiceComment supports English, Arabic, and French speech recognition.

Your selected language preference is saved locally on your device to provide a convenient experience when using the app.

We do not send this preference to our own servers.

6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

VoiceComment does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

However, the Android speech recognition service may process voice recordings or related information as described in Section 2.

VoiceComment also provides an optional link to Ko-fi for users who wish to support the development of the application.

If you choose to visit Ko-fi, you will be subject to Ko-fi's own privacy policy and terms.

7. Data Storage and Security

VoiceComment does not maintain its own remote database or server for storing user information.

Language preferences are stored locally on your device.

Voice recordings are not independently stored on VoiceComment servers. However, the handling and retention of audio by a third-party speech recognition provider are governed by that provider's policies.

8. Children's Privacy

VoiceComment does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The application does not require account registration or the submission of personal details.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published at the same privacy policy address, with a revised update date.
